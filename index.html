      How to set up the Shortcut
      1. Find Health Samples — Type: Sleep, sorted by End Date, newest first. Set the date range to the last 24 hours.
      2. Filter to asleep stages only (Core, Deep, REM). Skip “In Bed” and “Awake” or the total will be inflated.
      3. Calculate the total duration and divide to get hours.
      4. Copy to Clipboard — just the number, e.g. 7.5.
      5. Open this app and tap Import from Shortcut.

      To backfill several nights at once, copy one per line as YYYY-MM-DD=hours. Health only reads while the phone is unlocked, so run the automation after you unlock rather than overnight.

                  When any habit slips

                  • Never miss twice. One missed day has no measurable effect on how the habit forms. Two in a row is what turns a slip into a stop.
                  • Shrink it before you drop it. If you're missing a habit repeatedly, the behaviour is too big. One page, two minutes, one surface. A small rep still counts as a rep; a skipped day doesn't.
                  • Don't change the cue. Automaticity comes from the same trigger in the same context, over and over. Moving the cue restarts the clock.
                  • Early reps matter most. The automaticity curve is steepest at the start, so the first few weeks buy more than the later ones. Protect them, and expect it to feel effortful for longer than you'd like.
                  • Expect months, not weeks. Median time to automatic is about two months, and the spread runs from under three weeks to well over a year. If it still feels like effort at day 40, that's normal, not failure.
